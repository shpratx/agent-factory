## ✅ SWOT Analysis

**Strengths**
- Combines compliance-facilitation tooling with direct foodservice-buyer
  discovery — no reviewed competitor does both.
- Targets a segment (independent producers) actively being squeezed by the
  reviewed wholesalers' consolidation, rather than competing head-on for the
  same large-account customers.

**Weaknesses**
- No existing brand trust with either producers or foodservice buyers.
- Regulatory/legal structuring runway (assessed separately in
  `regulatory-feasibility.md`) is a dependency before any trade can be
  facilitated with confidence.

**Opportunities**
- Foodservice buyers' growing appetite for traceable, locally-sourced
  ingredients is a favourable demand-side backdrop.
- Independent producers' growing squeeze from wholesaler consolidation
  creates demand for an alternative channel that doesn't require joining a
  large wholesaler's catalogue.

**Threats**
- Bidfood or Brakes could bundle a "producer marketplace" feature into their
  existing compliance infrastructure faster than HarvestLink can reach scale.
- Regulatory ambiguity about facilitation-vs-Food-Business-Operator status
  (see `regulatory-feasibility.md`) could slow legal sign-off relative to an
  incumbent that already holds full FBO registration.

Sufficient — 4 competitor types reviewed (two major foodservice wholesalers,
one retail-focused wholesaler, and the independent/regional distributor long
tail), covering the full set of alternatives available to both producers and
foodservice buyers today.
