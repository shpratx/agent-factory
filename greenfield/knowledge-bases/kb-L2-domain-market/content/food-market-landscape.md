# Food Production & Distribution — Market Landscape

## Distribution Channel Types
- Foodservice/HORECA wholesale — hotels, restaurants, catering (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Retail grocery distribution — supermarkets, convenience stores (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Direct-to-consumer (DTC) — subscription boxes, farm-to-door delivery (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Dark-kitchen / delivery-only food production (→ Competitor Matrix)

## Known UK Market Player Categories (illustrative, not exhaustive — verify current status)
- Major foodservice wholesalers: Bidfood, Brakes (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Major grocery wholesalers/distributors: Booker (Tesco-owned), Costco wholesale (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Meal-kit / DTC subscription: Gousto, HelloFresh (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Local-sourcing DTC platforms: historically high-churn segment, notable exits — verify before citing a specific company as active (→ Competitor Matrix)
- Independent/regional distributors: fragmented long tail, no single dominant regional player (→ Competitor Matrix)

## Industry Trends
- Post-Brexit customs/border friction increased EU-ingredient import lead times (→ SWOT: Threats)
- Rising consumer demand for traceability/provenance data (→ SWOT: Opportunities)
- Growth in plant-based and allergen-free product lines (→ SWOT: Opportunities)
- Supply-chain resilience (multi-sourcing, buffer stock) prioritised since COVID-19 (→ SWOT: Opportunities/Threats)
- Consolidation pressure on independents from larger wholesalers' scale economics (→ SWOT: Threats)

## Market Sizing (illustrative estimates only — verify against current market-research before citing)
- TAM: UK foodservice/wholesale distribution market ~£30-35bn annual revenue (→ Market Sizing)
- SAM: independent/regional producer + HORECA buyer spend ~£2-4bn addressable (→ Market Sizing)
- SOM: compliance-enabled marketplace entrant, ~£5-15m achievable revenue in years 1-2 (→ Market Sizing)
- Producer-to-foodservice B2B marketplace/matching segment overall ~£150-300m today (→ Market Sizing)
- Independent/regional producer population: ~15,000-20,000 UK businesses (illustrative count) (→ Market Sizing)

## Customer Insights
- Foodservice buyers require documented compliance evidence before onboarding a new supplier (→ Customer Insights)
- Producer onboarding/procurement cycles typically run 4-8 weeks including compliance review (→ Customer Insights)
- Producers cite HACCP/allergen/traceability paperwork as their top onboarding friction point (→ Customer Insights)
- Buyers commonly demand 30-60 day payment terms; producers often need faster cycles (→ Customer Insights)
- Producers report cash-flow strain from long payment terms as a recurring complaint (→ Customer Insights)
- Buyers weight traceability/provenance documentation above price when vetting new suppliers (→ Customer Insights)
- Small producers lack dedicated admin staff to manage multi-buyer compliance paperwork (→ Customer Insights)

## Cost Structure Norms
- Cold-chain logistics is a major fixed-cost driver for distributors (→ SWOT: Weaknesses for new entrant)
- Thin margins typical in wholesale food distribution; volume drives profitability, not markup (→ SWOT: Weaknesses for new entrant)
- Commission-per-transaction (~2-5%) is the dominant pricing model for B2B food marketplaces (→ Pricing Benchmarks)
- Flat monthly subscription and per-listing fee are less common alternative models (→ Pricing Benchmarks)
- Thin wholesale margins limit viable commission rates for a new entrant (→ SWOT: Weaknesses for new entrant / Pricing Benchmarks)
